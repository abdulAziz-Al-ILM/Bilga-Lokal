# Bilga privacy policy — draft for product-owner review

Bilga is provided by ILM Mizan. This draft describes the current source, dated 2026-11-23, and must be reviewed against the final release.

Your notes, tasks, habits, journals and attachments are stored on your device. Ordinary use and text lookup work offline without a mandatory account. Local text lookup searches saved records on your device. Bilga does not include an advertising or analytics service.

Voice commands are optional and require microphone permission. Listening starts when you tap the microphone or start a conversation. Conversation mode can start the next listening turn while the voice screen is open. You can stop listening or leave the screen. Bilga does not create or retain an audio recording. In device-only mode Bilga requests Android's on-device recognition, which depends on language and device support. If you choose the system recognition option and accept its disclosure, Android's installed speech provider may send your speech to its servers. The provider's own policies apply. Bilga processes the returned text on your device. You can review the text, and a confirmed command may save it as part of a record you choose to create or edit. Spoken replies use an installed offline voice or the built-in local synthesizer.

Local databases and attachments use encryption tied to Android Keystore. Optional app locking uses the device credential or biometric capabilities; Bilga does not receive your biometric template. Encryption does not guarantee recovery after key loss or uninstall. Keep a portable encrypted backup and its password safe. We cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

When you choose Google Drive backup, Google account authorization and the selected encrypted backup are sent to Google using the app's private Drive folder. This is optional. The current implementation requires local backup for attachments rather than uploading an incomplete media backup. Google processes account and service information under its own policies.

If you choose to purchase, Google Play handles payment. Bilga checks purchase receipts and stores verification evidence locally. A configured purchase-verification service receives only purchase metadata (package/product, signed receipt and random installation binding), not your notes or journal contents. The owner must identify the actual verifier operator, retention and contact information before activating that service. Verified cached access can work offline. Google Play processes payment information under its policies.

You may read and export saved records after the 40-day trial ends. Trial expiry never deletes your data. Creating and editing after expiry requires a one-time lifetime purchase; no automatic charge occurs. Plain-text/file export creates an unencrypted copy at your chosen destination; protect or delete that copy yourself.

Android permissions support internet access for requested Drive, purchase and optional model download operations, microphone access for voice commands, device authentication, notifications, reboot rescheduling and media playback. Exact alarms and panels over other apps are optional user-controlled reminder capabilities. Standard notifications can work without overlay permission. Android battery and permission policies can affect timing. Widgets display only explicitly permitted content and can expose that content to launchers or people viewing your screen.

You control local deletion and optional backups. Deleting an in-app item may move it to trash; permanent deletion and copies in external exports, launchers or Drive are separate operations. Disconnecting Google does not by itself remove an already uploaded backup. This app does not activate public journal sharing.

Contact shown in the existing application: ilmmizanofficial@gmail.com. The owner must confirm the legal entity/contact, final verifier operation, regional disclosures, retention and store Data Safety answers before publication.

# Image assets

Method: built-in ImageGen, one generated landscape per theme, no reference photographs.
Place the resulting original PNGs in `tool/media/originals/<theme>.png` to rebuild using `create_media.py` (requires Python, numpy and FFmpeg). Rebuilding is optional; ready media is included.

Shared direction: Photorealistic natural cinematic landscape, 1536×1024, soft realistic lighting, atmospheric depth, center-crop safe; no people, UI, writing, logos, frame or watermark.

- forest: ancient emerald cedar forest, mossy stream, soft dawn sun shafts through silver morning mist, green and sage.
- emerald: crystalline green alpine lake, pine shores, distant peaks, small waterfall among mossy rocks, reflections and morning mist.
- ruby: authentic Japanese autumn garden, ruby-red maple canopy, pond, stone lantern, dark jade moss, subtle dawn mist; no pink cherry blossoms.
- ocean: secluded turquoise cove, cream beach, distant rocky headland, quiet dawn sky, gentle waves, navy and aquamarine.
- amethyst: layered mountains and calm lake at blue hour, violet lavender sky, subtle stars, pine silhouettes, muted plum and silver.
- amber: quiet path among golden autumn birches, still mountain lake reflecting golden late afternoon light, honey and ochre palette.
- mono: monochrome mountain lake at dawn, fog among pine forests and rugged peaks, mirror reflection, dark wet stones and soft silver mist.
